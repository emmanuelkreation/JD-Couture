The Craft

Four steps.
No shortcuts.

What happens between the idea and the box on your doorstep.

Step 01

Sketch

Every piece starts on paper. Silhouette first, then the fabric that will hold it. Nothing is cut before the drawing is right.

Step 02

Select

Fabrics are chosen for drape, not just colour. Satin that moves. Aso-oke that holds its shape. Lining that does not itch at 9pm.

Step 03

Cut

Measured to you. Shoulder, bust, waist, hip, length — taken properly, once, and kept on file for next time.

Step 04

Finish

Hand-checked before it leaves the studio. Seams, hem, buttons, zip. If it is not right, it does not go out.
